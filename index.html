Content Strategy

Five Pillars.
One Voice.

Creative direction is not a deliverable we produce at the end — it is the foundation every piece of content is built on. BULL speaks with earned authority: understated, factual, provenance-led. No hype. No filler words. The product earns the attention.

Brand Tone — The Brenaissance

Understated Provenance-led Factual Confident No hype Earned authority Farm-rooted Premium without pretension
01
Founder Storytelling
Tom is the brand. His background — the stud farm, the decision to build BULL, the refusal to compromise on quality — is the most powerful asset we have. We put him on camera and let the truth do the work. No scripted marketing speak. Real answers to direct questions.
Hook Ideas
  • "I grew up on this farm. Here's why that matters."
  • "The ear-tag stays on until I'm satisfied with the cut."
  • "Why I built a butchery before I built a brand."
  • "What 20 years of farming taught me about beef."
02
Product Truth & Provenance
Ear-tags, cuts, the curing process, what makes this beef different from supermarket shelf biltong. We show the process — from farm to packaging — without over-explaining. The footage makes the case.
Hook Ideas
  • "Every piece has a number. Here's what it means."
  • "This is what grass-fed actually looks like."
  • "From ear-tag to packaging in under 60 seconds."
  • "Most biltong has six ingredients it can't name."
03
Health & Lifestyle Benefits
32g protein per 100g. No artificial fillers. Grass-fed. High iron and zinc bioavailability. These are facts — we state them plainly and let the numbers do the persuading. Target: active adults, performance-conscious eaters, clean-label consumers.
Hook Ideas
  • "32 grams of protein. One ingredient list."
  • "Your post-gym snack shouldn't need a chemistry degree."
  • "Clean label is not a trend. It's the baseline."
  • "High protein. No powder. No compromise."
04
Scroll-Stop Hook Content
Researched hooks built for the target audience — people who follow food quality, fitness, and provenance content. Fast, factual, designed to stop the scroll in the first two seconds. No trend-chasing. Hooks that hold up in six months.
Hook Ideas
  • "We know this animal's birthday."
  • "South Africa's most traceable biltong. Now open."
  • "Watch it sliced. Know where it came from."
  • "Not all biltong is equal. Here's the proof."
05
Offers & Promotions
Launch-week pricing, loyalty signals, and opening day offers. Communicated plainly — no artificial urgency, no inflated RRP. The offer is real, the deadline is real, and the product justifies itself beyond the discount.
Hook Ideas
  • "Opening week. Launch price. First come."
  • "One week only — then the price is the price."
  • "Come in. Taste it. Decide."
  • "We don't run promotions. Except this once."
